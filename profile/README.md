# hyperfocus.tech

## First 360° video intelligence platform

AI-powered short form video analysis for brands in social commerce.

---

### Core Capabilities

- **Brand Category Analysis** — Multi-modal understanding of category video trends, hook mechanics, retention drivers, and product display patterns.
- **Competitors Overview** — Systematic competitive benchmarking across creative velocity, visual messaging, and market share of attention.
- **Scenario Generation** — Turning multi-modal video intelligence into camera-ready scripts, hooks, and production shoot packages on a single screen.
